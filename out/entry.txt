Leave the stool. Yes, that one. I carved it from Saint Orra's gallows, and it has served me better than her priests. Tomorrow, when my salt knights reach the wells, the city will kneel. Bring me the keys, the prince, and... the keys first. Set them there. No trumpets, even if he brings the crown. The last man's jaw still hangs above the south gate. Tell the prince his people may stand when I rise to grant him terms. Pull that door shut. Softly. There. Set his plea beside the rest. I shall consider it.
Forgiveness may take years.
